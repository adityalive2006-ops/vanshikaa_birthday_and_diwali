# A Little World for Vanshikaa 🪔✨
### 20th Birthday & Diwali Celebration • 8 November 2026

A bespoke, responsive web experience designed to celebrate **Vanshikaa's 20th Birthday**, coinciding with the festival of **Diwali, 8 November 2026** (born 8 November 2006).

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Architecture
- **Palette:** Deep Velvet Plum (`#08020e`, `#120422`), Marigold & Blush Rose (`#f43f5e`, `#fb7185`), and Champagne Gold (`#fbbf24`, `#f59e0b`).
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel, Playfair Display, Plus Jakarta Sans, and Caveat.
- **Lighting & Ambiance:** Warm candlelight/diya illumination, natural flickering flames, and subtle particle sparkles.

---

## 🏛️ Website Experience

1. **The Birthday Portrait & Live Moment:**
   - Editorial arched portrait frame featuring Vanshikaa's photo.
   - High-definition square looping video medallion isolating her smiling, joyful face.
   - Milestone celebratory countdown and confetti burst.

2. **The Birthday Letter:**
   - Handcrafted royal envelope with golden filigree and embossed wax seal **V**.
   - Opens into a textured deckle-edge ivory parchment card with thoughtful birthday and Diwali wishes.

3. **Words of Celebration:**
   - Handpicked birthday reflections and Diwali blessings carousel with one-click copy.

4. **The Courtyard Arena (Games):**
   - **Diwali Tic-Tac-Toe:** Player vs Player & smart AI with Easy and Medium difficulty modes, Diwali markers (🪔 vs ✨), and live scoreboard.
   - **Snake & Ladder:** Classic 100-square board for 2 to 4 local players with 3D rolling dice, step-by-step token movement, functional golden ladders, and mischievous snakes.
   - **Royal Ludo:** Mathematically authentic 15×15 cross-and-circle Ludo board, 2 to 4 players, local Pass & Play or AI opponent, 4 tokens per player, rolling 6 to unlock, safe star zones, token captures, and home path runs.

5. **The Festival of Lights:**
   - **The Eight Diyas:** Interactive brass thali with 8 earthen lamps you can light one-by-one or all at once with realistic flickering flames and sound.
   - **The Golden Rangoli:** Rotating geometric mandala art with 3 live color themes.
   - **Night Sky Fireworks:** Interactive canvas where clicking or tapping launches colorful sparkler rockets and fireworks.

6. **The 20-Candle Cake (Grand Finale):**
   - 3-tier frosted cake on an ambient glowing pedestal.
   - Exactly 20 glowing interactive candles that can be blown out individually or all at once, triggering celebratory confetti and the birthday wish card.
